Privacy Policy — Akikoi (Lent Items Tracker)

Last updated: August 6, 2026

In short: Akikoi collects no personal data. The app runs entirely on your device, with no user account and no server.

1. Data stored by the app

Everything you enter (items, borrower names, dates, photos, notes, amounts) is stored only in your phone's local storage. It is never transmitted to the developer or to third parties. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

2. Permissions

Camera and photo library access are used only to attach a picture to a loan, at your request; images never leave your device. Notifications are used only for the return-date reminders you set.

3. In-app purchase

The one-time unlock is processed by Google Play and our purchase-management provider (RevenueCat). An anonymous technical identifier and transaction details are processed by these services under their own policies. Akikoi never has access to your payment information.

4. Advertising and tracking

Akikoi contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.

5. Children

Akikoi collects no data from anyone, including minors.

6. Changes

Any update to this policy will be published on this page.
